Our Forum

About FOSIECON

The Forum of State Independent Electoral Commissions of Nigeria (FOSIECON) is meant to bring together under one umbrella the 36 State Independent Electoral Commissions for easy communication and planning on electoral processes, in line with the Constitution and the Electoral Act 2026. Established in 2000, the Forum supports individual State Electoral Commissions on issues of Local Government elections and shares ideas on the best practices across board.

Chairman of the Forum

Hon. Mamman Nda Eri

What We Offer

Our Services

FOSIECON exists to coordinate and strengthen the work of Nigeria's State Independent Electoral Commissions.

🗳️

Electoral Management Body Support

Coordinating the 36 State Independent Electoral Commissions as the recognized national electoral management forum.

👁️

Observation of Elections

Independent observation of State Local Government elections to promote credibility and public trust.

📡

Monitoring of Elections

Ongoing monitoring of electoral processes across member states, in line with the Constitution and the Electoral Act 2026.

🤝

Support for Sister States

Technical and peer support for member commissions during their own election cycles.

📚

Best-Practice Sharing

A platform for member commissions to exchange ideas and best practices across board.

🏛️

Liaison with INEC & Partners

Strengthening FOSIECON's association with INEC and other stakeholders on election matters nationally.
